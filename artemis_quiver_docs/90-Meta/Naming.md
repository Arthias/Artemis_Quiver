# Naming Lore — Artemis Quiver

> The app's identity is built on the Greek myth of **Artemis**, goddess of the hunt, and her **quiver** of arrows.

## Core metaphor

| Term | Meaning | App equivalent |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Artemis** | Goddess of the hunt | The app — guides your job hunt |
| **Quiver** | Container for arrows | The toolset (CV builder, cover letter gen, match scorer, interview simulator) |
| **Arrow** | A single projectile | A job application (take aim → fire) |
| **Hunt** | The active pursuit | Your job search campaign |

## Extended metaphors (use sparingly)

| Term | Meaning | When to use |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Bow** | The force that launches arrows | The LLM provider (primary/secondary model slots) |
| **Hone the arrow** | Refine before firing | Profile editing, CV/CL refinement |
| **Sharpen the tip** | Prepare for impact | Interview prep, STAR responses |
| **Fletching** | Stabilizes the arrow in flight | Cover letter + CV alignment |
| **Quarry** | The target being hunted | A specific job/company |

## Tone guidelines

- Lead with clarity — the metaphor should illuminate, not obscure
- Use sparingly in UI labels (tooltips, empty states, onboarding) — don't force it onto every button
- The tagline "**Take aim at your next role**" anchors the metaphor at the top level
- Avoid inside-baseball references that confuse new users
